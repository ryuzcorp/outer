## What we store and why

We store secrets and codes as hashes or encrypted values, never in readable form. API keys, OAuth tokens, and sign-in codes are stored as hashes; app secret values are encrypted and write-only.

## Processors

We do not sell your data. Three processors handle it on our behalf:

- **Cloudflare** — edge network, DNS, DDoS protection, and TLS for `outer.now` and every app host; R2 object storage in the EU for code bundles, app data, snapshots, and exports; and the Email Service that delivers sign-in codes and notifications from `Outer <hello@outer.now>`.
- **Stripe** — payments, subscription management, and tax calculation at checkout. Your email and billing details go to Stripe when you upgrade or manage billing.
- **Jev, through Cloudflare** — content classification for the pre-live gate and after-live checks. Extracted app text (page copy, content files, seed strings, the slug and app name) is classified by Typesafe Jev on Workers AI through the AI Gateway, with log collection off. No file contents beyond the extracted text go to the classifier. Naming these processors in plain terms is required by our spec, and consenting to the [Terms](/terms) covers this processing.

## How long we keep it

| Data | Kept |
| --- | --- |
| Logs and errors history | 24 hours on Free, 30 days on Pro |
| Deploys and their log tails | Last 50 per app |
| Database backups | Daily dumps, keep 3 on Free and 30 on Pro with any-day restore |
| Code exports | Auto-deleted 24 hours after creation |
| Inactive Free apps | Deleted after 90 days without owner activity, with reminders at 30, 7, and 1 day before; 7-day restore window after the stop, then purged |
| Stopped apps after a downgrade | Purged 30 days later, with a warning email around day 23 |
| Accounts you delete | Removed with every owned app; collaborations stay with their owners |

Platform state snapshots and bucket mirrors are also kept on the operator's disk for disaster recovery. When retention ends, the data is deleted or irreversibly anonymized.

## Cookies

Outer sets one strictly necessary cookie: `__Host-outer_sid`, a 30-day sign-in session. It cannot be set or overwritten by any app, and it carries no tracking. The docs site runs no analytics and sets no marketing cookies. Your apps may set their own cookies; those belong to the app owner, not to us.

## Your rights (GDPR)

You are in the EU/EEA or use the service from there, so the GDPR applies. You may:

- **Access** — see your email, plan, usage, and reset dates in Console → Account, or ask your agent for `whoami`. See your connections and API keys under Connections & keys.
- **Portability** — download any owned app as a zip at any commit (Console → Settings → Export code, or `export_code`). See [Own your code and data](/security/data-ownership).
- **Correction** — update what you can in the Console, or write to [OWNER: legal contact email] for the rest.
- **Deletion** — delete your account in Console → Account → delete-account. Apps you own go with it.
- **Withdraw consent and object** — revoke any OAuth grant or API key any time; unsubscribe from non-critical emails with the one-click link in each message.
- **Complain** — to us first at [OWNER: legal contact email], or to your supervisory authority ([OWNER: lead authority, e.g. the Polish UODO]).

We answer requests within one month. Security-critical notices (failed sign-ins, taken-down apps, purge warnings) have no unsubscribe because they protect your account.

## Security

One app cannot reach another app's data: each app runs under its own user id with its own limits, its storage credentials are scoped to its own prefix and expire on their own, and network egress blocks private ranges and SMTP. The netcup host sits behind Cloudflare's edge with origin lock, and the platform database is snapshotted to the bucket. No measure is perfect, and a prototype carries residual risk — that is part of what the beta is validating.
