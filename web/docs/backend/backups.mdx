---
title: Back up and restore your data
description: Daily database dumps run after activity and keep 3 days (30 on Pro with any-day restore); back up, download, or restore from the Console.
---

An app with a database gets a daily dump automatically once it has been active in the prior 24 hours. Dumps are full `.sql` exports, stored aside from your app's files. They keep 3 days, or 30 on [Pro](/account/plans) with any-day restore.

## How to manage backups

1. Open your app in the [Console](/get-started/console-tour).
2. Go to **Data → D1 → Backups**.
3. Use **Back up now** for an on-demand dump, **Download** to save one as `.sql`, or **Restore** to replace the live tables with a dump.

:::note
Restore takes a fresh backup first, behind a confirmation naming that backup. A bad restore stays restorable.
:::

For the technical detail, your builder reads [Back up the database](/agents/backend/backups).
