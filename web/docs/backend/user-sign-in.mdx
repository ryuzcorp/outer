Your app can have its own sign-in, backed by your app's database. Offer email sign-in, Google, GitHub, or a mix.

## How to add sign-in

Ask your builder for the providers you want, such as "let people sign in with email and Google". Sign-in pages and protected pages come with the request.

OAuth providers need credentials from each provider's developer console. The callback URL must point at your `<slug>.outer.now` host.

## What happens with secrets

Sign-in needs a signing secret plus one key pair per OAuth provider. Your builder stores them outside the code before the first sign-in deploy, and they apply on the next deploy.

Test sign-in on the live link, not only locally. OAuth callbacks must match the app's public host.

Keep API keys in [Secrets](/security/secrets), never in code.

For the technical detail, your builder reads [Auth](/agents/backend/auth).
