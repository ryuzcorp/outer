title: Send email from your app
description: Apps have no built-in mailer and SMTP is blocked; send through Resend over HTTP with your own key.
---

Your app has no built-in mailer. Outbound SMTP ports (25, 465, 587) are blocked, so apps send mail through an HTTP email service such as Resend, with your own key.

## How to set it up

1. Create a Resend account and verify a sender domain you control.
2. Ask your builder to store your key as a [Secret](/security/secrets) — never in code.
3. Ask for the emails you want: invites, receipts, digests.

Your builder validates recipient addresses before sending. Bounced spam burns your domain, so keep lists clean.

There is no inbox path into `<slug>.outer.now`. Receiving mail needs a service in front that forwards to your app as HTTP.

For the technical detail, your builder reads [Email](/agents/backend/email).
