Your app gets one file bucket (R2) for uploads like images and documents. It shares the app with your single database — a second bucket fails the deploy.

## What to know about uploads

Uploaded images and video skip text review, so ask for your own checks when strangers can upload. Never serve uploads as executable content.

## How to browse your files

1. Open your app in the [Console](/get-started/console-tour).
2. Go to **Data → R2**.
3. Browse folders, preview images and text, download, upload, or delete files.

Reads use the same access as your app. Writes are recorded in the activity log.

For the technical detail, your builder reads [Files](/agents/backend/files).
