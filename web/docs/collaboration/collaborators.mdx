---
title: Work with collaborators
description: Invite up to 10 collaborators per Pro app by email.
---

Collaborators are a Pro feature, with up to 10 per app. Only the app owner can invite or remove them, in Console → Settings → Collaborators. The next pages cover sharing your link and exporting your code.

1. Enter the collaborator's email.
2. They get an invite email admitting that address to the beta.
3. The app appears in their list once they sign in.

Code changes a collaborator makes charge to your daily quota as the owner. If you downgrade, collaborators stay listed but their access pauses until you re-upgrade.
