---
title: Work with collaborators
description: Invite collaborators to your app by email — 1 per app on Free, up to 10 on Pro.
---

Free includes 1 collaborator per app; Pro raises that to 10. Only the app owner can invite or remove them, in Console → Settings → Collaborators. The next pages cover sharing your link and exporting your code.

1. Enter the collaborator's email.
2. They get an invite email admitting that address to the beta.
3. The app appears in their list once they sign in.

Code changes a collaborator makes charge to your daily quota as the owner. If you downgrade, collaborators beyond 1 per app become read-only.
