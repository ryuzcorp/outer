---
title: Share your app
description: Share a link, text, and QR code — unlisted by default, public on Pro.
---

Ask your agent to share your app and it returns the URL, a one-line share text, and a QR code.

New apps are unlisted: anyone with the link can open them, but search engines stay out. Public apps are listed and indexable, and public visibility is a Pro feature. Only the owner can change visibility in Console → Settings.

If you downgrade, public apps return to unlisted.

Free apps show a small outer.now badge in the bottom-right corner of its pages. Pro removes it.

Preview detail for click-throughs lives in [Preview changes](/testing/preview-deploys).
