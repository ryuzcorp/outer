Most people open your app on a phone. Every page should work at 360px wide before it looks good on desktop. The next pages cover themes and the words your visitors read.

## What to ask for

- Layouts that stack on phones and spread out from small screens up.
- No fixed widths wider than the screen; long text truncates or wraps, and wide tables scroll sideways.
- Big tap targets: full-width inputs and standard buttons for primary actions.
- Pages sized to the visible phone screen, so browser bars never cut content off.

If a page feels cramped on your phone, say so with the model name. Your builder adjusts the layout.

For the technical detail, your builder reads [Style with daisyUI](/agents/design/styling).
