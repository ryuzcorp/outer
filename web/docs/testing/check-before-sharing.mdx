---
title: Check before sharing
description: Look at each change in a real page before you send the link.
---

Ask your agent to look at the app after each change. A clean deploy proves the build passed, not that the page looks right.

1. Tell your agent what page to open and what you changed.
2. Ask what it sees: layout, text, and any errors.
3. Fix what looks wrong before you share the link.

Your agent renders the live page with screenshots and console checks. How it works: [Look](/agents/testing/look).

:::note
If the page shows a "paused / building / held" screen, the app has no successful deploy yet, is paused over quota, or is held by moderation. Ask your agent for the deploy status.
:::
