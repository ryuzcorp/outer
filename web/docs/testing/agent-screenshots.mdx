---
title: Agent screenshots
description: Ask your agent for a screenshot of any page, phone or desktop.
---

Your agent can open any page of your app and show you what it looks like. Ask after each change you care about.

1. Name the page (the home page works when you name none).
2. Name the device: phone (390 wide) or desktop (1280 wide).
3. Ask for the whole page when you need below the fold.

| Choice | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| Phone | A 390-wide view, like a phone screen. |
| Desktop | A 1280-wide view, the default. |
| Whole page | The full scroll, not only the viewport. |

Your agent loads your app's own address, so unlisted apps work. Each capture also returns the page status and title, plus console errors (up to 50) and failed requests (up to 50). When the recorder cannot run, your agent says why and checks the server logs instead. Screenshots are one step of [checking your app before sharing](/testing/check-before-sharing). Detail for the curious: [Look](/agents/testing/look).

:::note
Build output lives on the deploy, not in screenshots. For a failed deploy, open the deploy in [Console](/get-started/console-tour) or ask your agent for the log tail.
:::
