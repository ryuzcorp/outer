---
title: Account settings
description: Email, connections, keys, usage, and deleting your account.
---

Your account page holds your email and terms, your connections and API keys, and your usage against quota with reset dates. `whoami` shows the same usage over MCP.

Revoke a client or a key any time in Console → Connections & keys. Secrets are never shown again after creation.

To delete your account:

1. Open Console → Account → delete-account.
2. Type your account email to confirm.

Deleting removes your owned apps. Your code and data stay exportable first: [Own your code and data](/security/data-ownership). Agent reference: [Limits and quotas](/deploy/limits).
