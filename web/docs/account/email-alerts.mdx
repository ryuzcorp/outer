---
title: Get email alerts
description: Deploy, error, quota, and inactivity emails with per-user toggles.
---

Outer emails you when a deploy fails, when errors spike, when a quota hits 80% or 100%, and before a Free app is deleted for inactivity (30, 7, and 1 day out).

1. Open Console → your account → notifications.
2. Toggle each alert on or off.

All alerts default to on, per person. Every mail carries a one-click unsubscribe link. Turning off inactivity reminders stops the deadline emails; the countdown and the deletion still apply.
