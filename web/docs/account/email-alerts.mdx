---
title: Get email alerts
description: Deploy, error, and quota emails with per-user toggles.
---

Outer emails you when a deploy fails, when errors spike, and when a quota hits 80% or 100%.

1. Open Console → your account → notifications.
2. Toggle each alert on or off.

All alerts default to on, per person. Every mail carries a one-click unsubscribe link.

Agent reference: [Follow the deploy pipeline](/agents/deploy/pipeline).
