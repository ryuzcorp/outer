---
title: Manage billing
description: Upgrade through Stripe Checkout and manage the subscription in the portal.
---

Billing is live. Upgrade from the Console Plan card's Upgrade button, or ask your agent — both open a Stripe Checkout page. Your plan lifts on the next call after payment.

1. Press Upgrade in the Console, or ask your agent to upgrade.
2. Complete Stripe Checkout.
3. Make any call — your plan now reads Pro.

Manage the subscription (update the card, cancel) from the Plan card's Manage billing button. A missed payment starts a 7-day grace period as Pro, with emails on day 0 and day 5. Outer also emails you about failed deploys, error spikes, and quota use: [Email alerts](/account/email-alerts).

Downgrading returns you to Free: your oldest app stays live and the rest stop, stamped with the date. Stopped apps are purged 30 days later, with a warning email around day 23. The downgrade also deletes every custom hostname and returns public apps to unlisted. Code and data stay, but Pro behaviors pause: collaborator access and keep-warm apply again on resubscribe, and a deploy using Pro-only bindings fails with a clear error until you remove them. Re-upgrading before day 30 cancels the purge but restarts nothing.

Agent reference: [Deploy limits](/deploy/limits).
