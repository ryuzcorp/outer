Outer has two plans: Free and Pro at $8/month. Early users pay $4/month through a 50% launch discount while the promo runs.

The discount applies as a Stripe coupon at checkout, automatically. Launch subscribers keep the $4 price while subscribed. Stripe Tax calculates tax at checkout.

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apps owned | 1 | 10 |
| Code changes per day (UTC) | 50 | 500 |
| Requests, storage, traffic | 250k, 500 MB, 5 GB | 5M, 25 GB, 100 GB |
| Sleep | 30 min idle | 24 h idle, plus keep-warm |
| Collaborators, export, custom domains | — | Up to 10 per app, zip export, one hostname per app |

Full numbers live in [limits and quotas](/deploy/limits). Billing detail lives in [the deploy pipeline](/agents/deploy/pipeline).
