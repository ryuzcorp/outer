title: Choose a plan
description: Free and Pro compared, plus the launch discount.
---

Outer has two plans: Free and Pro at $8/month. Early users pay $4/month through a 50% launch discount while the promo runs. The next pages cover billing, alerts, and settings.

The discount applies as a Stripe coupon at checkout, automatically, while the promotion runs. The owner ends it in Stripe; existing subscribers keep whatever Stripe says. Stripe Tax calculates tax at checkout.

| | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apps owned | 2 | 20 |
| Code changes per day (UTC) | 40 | 1000 (fair use) |
| Build minutes per day | 30 | 300 |
| Sleep | Sleeps after 15 min idle | Keep-warm; without keep-warm, 30 min |
| Visibility | Unlisted only | Public + indexable |
| Visitor access (PIN/password) | — | PIN or password gate on every host and path |
| Custom domains, preview deploys | — | One hostname per app, preview deploys |
| Code export | Yes, everyone (owner only) | Yes |
| "outer.now" badge | Small badge, bottom-right of its pages | Removed — clean pages on Pro |
| Logs and errors | 24 hours | 30 days |
| Backups | Daily, keep 3 | Daily, keep 30 + any-day restore |
| Database / files | 100 MB / 250 MB | 5 GB / 25 GB |
| Requests per month | Soft 100k (email + upgrade ask, keeps serving) | Soft 5M (never pause a live app) |
| Deleted after inactivity | 90 days without owner activity, after reminders | Never while subscribed |

Storage caps refuse writes at the cap and never delete. Downgrade keeps your 2 oldest apps live; the rest stop but are retained for 30 days. An app access gate stays enforced until you turn it off — you just can't set a new one on Free.

Free apps are deleted after 90 days without owner activity. You get reminder emails at 30, 7, and 1 day before the deadline; signing in or asking your agent to do anything restores the countdown, and so does a collaborator working on the app. Visiting the app doesn't count. `whoami`, `list_apps`, and `get_app` show the deadline as `deletes_at`, and the Console shows it on the apps list and the app overview.

Full numbers live in [limits and quotas](/deploy/limits). Billing detail lives in [Manage billing](/account/billing).
