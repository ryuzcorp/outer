Two plans. Every number is enforced in code; `whoami` shows your usage and
when it resets.

## Free

- 1 app owned.
- 50 code changes a day (UTC). `create_app`'s template commit and `rollback` never count.
- 60 build minutes a day, 1 concurrent build.
- 512 MiB fleet memory, 0.5 CPU, sleep after 30 min idle.
- 250k requests a month, 500 MB app storage, 5 GB outbound traffic a month.
- Bindings: D1, KV, Durable Objects. Cron intervals of at least 1 h.
- 5 API keys. Logs and metrics kept 7 days.

## Pro — $8/month

- 10 apps owned.
- 500 code changes a day, 600 build minutes a day, 2 concurrent builds.
- 1 GiB fleet memory, 1 CPU, sleep after 24 h idle.
- 5M requests a month, 25 GB app storage, 100 GB outbound traffic a month.
- Bindings add R2 (file uploads), Queues and Workflows. Cron intervals down to 1 min.
- Collaborators (up to 10 per app), 20 API keys, 30-day retention.
- Code export ([guide](/guides/export)): the whole tree as a zip, at the
live version or any earlier commit — from `export_code` or Settings →
Export code. Owner only, links good for one hour.

## Billing

Pro is **$8/month**. Early users get a **50% launch discount ($4/month)**
through a Stripe coupon that's applied automatically at checkout while the
promo is on. Taxes are calculated by Stripe Tax at checkout. The only way
to pay is the Upgrade button (console → Plan) or the `upgrade` MCP tool:
both open a Stripe Checkout page, and the plan lifts on the next call
after payment. Nothing else upgrades an account.

Manage the subscription (update the card, cancel) from the Plan card's
Manage billing button or the `manage_billing` MCP tool: both open a
Stripe Customer Portal page.

::::note
A missed payment starts a **7-day grace period** as Pro (emails on day 0
and day 5), then the account returns to Free. On downgrade the **oldest
app stays live** and the rest stop (kept 30 days, then purged after a
warning). Code and data stay; Pro behaviors pause: public visibility,
collaborator access, custom domains and keep-warm apply again on
resubscribe, and a deploy using Pro-only bindings fails with a clear error
until they're removed.
::::
## When a limit is hit

Change, build, app and collaborator limits are hard refusals: the tool
returns `quota_exceeded` with an upgrade link and a `user_message` your agent
relays verbatim. Requests, storage and outbound traffic on Free pause the app
at 100% until the month resets — data is kept. Full table:
[Limits](/guides/limits).
