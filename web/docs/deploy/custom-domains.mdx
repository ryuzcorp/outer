---
title: Use a custom domain
description: Serve your Pro app from your own hostname with Cloudflare for SaaS.
---

Custom domains are a Pro feature, with one hostname per app. Only the app owner can manage them, in Console → Settings → Custom domain.

1. Add the domain in the Console card.
2. At your DNS provider, point a CNAME from your hostname to `domains.outer.now`.
3. Add the TXT ownership and certificate records exactly as shown.
4. Press Refresh until the status turns active.

| Status | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| pending | The hostname or certificate is not validated yet. Check the DNS records, then refresh. |
| active | The domain serves your app. |
| failed | Validation stalled. The reason names the fix — repair DNS and refresh, or remove and re-add. |

The status badges read live from Cloudflare. Subdomains work anywhere; an apex domain needs a provider with CNAME flattening.

Renaming your app keeps its domain, since the binding follows the app. Downgrading to Free or deleting the app removes it.
