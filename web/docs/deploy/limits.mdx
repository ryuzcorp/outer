---
title: Limits and quotas
description: The enforced quotas on Free and Pro, and what happens when you hit one.
---

Your agent sees the same numbers through `whoami`, which shows usage and reset dates.

| Quota | Free | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Apps owned | 2 | 20 |
| Code changes per day (UTC) | 40 | 1000 (fair use) |
| Build minutes per day | 30 | 300 |
| outer.now badge | Small badge, bottom-right of its pages | Removed — clean pages on Pro |
| Visibility | Unlisted only | Public + indexable |
| Custom domains per app | — | 1 |
| Sleep | Sleeps after 15 min idle (cold start on wake) | Keep-warm; without keep-warm, sleeps after 30 min |
| Collaborators per app | 1 | 10 |
| Active preview deploys | — | 1 per app |
| Logs and errors kept | 24 hours | 30 days |
| Backups | Daily, keep 3 | Daily, keep 30 + any-day restore |
| Database (D1) | 100 MB | 5 GB |
| Files (R2) | 250 MB | 25 GB |
| Requests per month | Soft 100k | Soft 5M |
| Code export | Yes, everyone (owner only) | Yes |
| Inactive deletion | After 90 days without activity, with reminders | Never while subscribed |

The template commit that creates your app and a rollback never count as code changes. Code changes charge to the app owner, whoever makes them.

When a quota is hit, the tool returns `quota_exceeded` with an upgrade link and a `user_message`. Ask your agent to relay that message word for word. Code changes reset at midnight UTC, and `whoami` shows your current counts and reset dates.

Storage caps refuse writes at the cap — nothing is ever deleted. Request caps are soft: at 100% you get an email plus an upgrade ask, and the app keeps serving. A live app is never paused over quota.

Downgrade keeps your 2 oldest apps live; the rest stop but are retained for 30 days. Collaborators beyond 1 per app become read-only.

Free apps are deleted after 90 days without owner activity, counted from your last sign-in, tool call, or Console visit — or a collaborator's tool call on that app. Visiting the app doesn't count. Reminder emails go out at 30, 7, and 1 day before the deadline. `whoami`, `list_apps`, and `get_app` show the deadline as `deletes_at`; after the deadline the app stops and its address serves an inactivity page for 7 more days, during which any sign-in or tool call restores it. Then it is purged: code, data, and files go, and the name becomes claimable again. Downgrading to Free restarts the 90-day clock on that date; extra apps stopped by the downgrade follow the 30-day stopped-app retention instead.

Enforcement detail lives in [Stay inside quotas and caps](/agents/platform/limits).
