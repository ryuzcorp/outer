# Brand and account-page fragments refused in app slugs (spec §7.1).
#
# One lowercase entry per line. Matching (see apps/slug.rs `brand_hit`):
# an entry hits when it is a whole hyphen-separated token of the slug, or —
# for entries of 5+ characters — when it appears anywhere in the slug as a
# substring. Short entries (aws, gov, irs, ups, dhl, …) are token-only, so
# ordinary words like "paws", "governor" or "cups" stay claimable.
#
# The substring rule deliberately over-refuses: "appleton", "snapple" and
# "pineapple" all contain "apple" and are refused. That is the documented
# tradeoff — err on refusing, and the agent picks another slug — because
# phishing slugs concatenate ("appleid", "paypalverify") far more often
# than legitimate apps embed a brand name.
#
# Phishing targets first: payment, tech-platform and account pages, then
# the carriers and agencies whose names carry seizure/delivery scams.
